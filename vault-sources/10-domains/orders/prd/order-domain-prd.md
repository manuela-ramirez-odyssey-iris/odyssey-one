## Consumers

* **Legacy TMS (post-renaps work):** Legacy TMS needs to interact with the order domain to achieve the same features/capability it currently has.
* **CX Platform:** Customers want the ability to view their order and related shipments. Customers sometimes share a shipment instead of an order with Odyssey, and in these cases a 'synthetic' order will be created to maintain the Order/Shipment structure and relationship. The synthetic order is merely a copy of the shipment the customer shared with Odyssey.
  * Customers want the ability to drill into each shipment on an order for further details (shipment status and tracking details).

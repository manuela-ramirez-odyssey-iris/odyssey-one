## Dave Schultz — consolidation and the prefix (asked 2026-09-15)

**Manuela asked:** "ah also, Consolidated shipments have their own identifiers or we use THE odyssey shipment identifier and treat it as a 'type of shipment'"

**Dave:**
> Consol or not Consol shouldn't make a difference. if you look in the DB on the shipment table, you will see shipment_id and odyssey_shipment_id. The shipment_id is the PK (no two rows have the same value) and you are already displaying that correctly in the UI. The odyssey_shipment_id is the one that is common across both buy and sell (it's always been in the database, we just need it on the UI)
>
> when you are creating a shipment, you know whether it only has one order or is a consolidation. So, at that moment, the correct prefix can be assigned. **A shipment cannot change from one type to another.**

Closes open question 1 below: the prefix is assigned once, at creation, and never re-derived.

## Dave Schultz — how consolidation actually happens (asked 2026-09-15)

Asked whether our Order Change flow could move a shipment across the 1↔2 order boundary, given "a shipment cannot change from one type to another".

**Dave:**
> that would be done by MOVING the loads from their current single order shipments over to a NEW shipment that is a consolidation
>
> (you can't add orders to a direct shipment, you create a NEW Consolidation shipment using the Loads from other Shipments…)
>
> and (because of NN integration) once a shipment is a consolidation, even if it goes back down to one order, it still has to be treated as a Consolidation (so, once something is a "C", it stays that way forever…)

**Three rules fall out:**
1. A direct (`O`) shipment can never gain orders. Consolidating means creating a NEW `C` shipment and moving loads into it.
2. A `C` shipment that drops back to one order **stays `C`** — forced by the NN integration, not a display preference.
3. Therefore the prefix is write-once, which is what our stored-not-computed implementation already does (DEC-147).

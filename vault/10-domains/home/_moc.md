# Home — Map of Content

Entry point for the Home domain knowledge base.

## What Home is

Home is **not a data-owning domain** — it's a cross-domain dashboard with customer-scoped widgets. It composes data from other domains (Orders, Shipments, Tracking) and does not have its own grooming sessions, decision log, or backlog the way Shipments does.

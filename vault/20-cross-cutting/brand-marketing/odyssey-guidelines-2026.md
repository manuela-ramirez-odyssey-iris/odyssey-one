Odyssey’s Angle
A Unique Element
Odyssey’s identity includes a forward-leaning
parallelogram as an important graphic
device. This shape is changeable in
proportion and can be used as a solid color
or a photographic container. The key element
is the angle of the diagonal sides.
As we’ll discuss later in this document, the
angle is also an important tool for developing
graphic layouts.
Born From the Logo
8.94°
The specific angle, 8.94°, is specified to
match the forward slant of Futura Bold Italic,
which is the typeface used to construct the
Odyssey logo.
Please be mindful of this angle as you
resize the parallelogram in layouts,
as non-proportional scaling will change
the corner angle measurements.
